CHAPTER 1 — INTRODUCTION & SCOPE

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1.1 Purpose
This Comprehensive Policy ("Policy") establishes the standards, rights, and
obligations governing employment at Zenith Corporation India Pvt. Ltd.
(hereinafter "Zenith" or "the Company"). The Policy is designed to ensure
full compliance with applicable Indian laws, including the Labour Codes 2020,
the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the POSH Act 2013, the
Maternity Benefit Act 1961 (as amended in 2017), and the Companies Act 2013.

1.2 Applicability
This Policy applies to all employees, whether permanent, contractual, or on
deputation, working in any of the Company's offices across India.

1.3 Review and Updates
This Policy shall be reviewed by the Legal and HR teams annually. Employees
will be notified of material changes via the internal HR portal.

CHAPTER 2 — COMPENSATION, BENEFITS & STATUTORY COMPLIANCE

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 Salary Payment
Wages are paid on or before the 7th day of the subsequent month via direct
bank transfer. Payslips are issued electronically no later than the date of
payment. The Company shall not make any deductions from salary except those
authorised under law or with the written consent of the employee.

2.2 Provident Fund
The Company deducts Provident Fund contributions at 12% of basic wages from
each employee's salary and deposits an equal employer contribution monthly
with the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO). PF passbooks are
accessible to employees via the EPFO member portal.

2.3 Employee State Insurance
All employees drawing a gross salary up to the applicable ESI threshold are
enrolled under the Employees' State Insurance scheme. Contributions are
deducted and deposited monthly as required under the ESI Act.

2.4 Gratuity
Employees completing 5 or more years of continuous service are entitled to
gratuity computed at 15 days' wages for each year of service, subject to the
Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.

2.5 Performance-Linked Rewards
Annual performance bonuses are determined by the Compensation Committee
based on individual KPIs, team performance, and overall company results.

CHAPTER 3 — LEAVE POLICY

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1 Annual Earned Leave
All confirmed employees are entitled to 24 days of paid earned leave per
calendar year. Up to 15 days of unused leave may be carried forward to the
next year. Leave encashment is permitted at the time of separation.

3.2 Casual and Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of casual leave and 10 days of sick leave
per year. Sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days requires a medical
certificate from a registered medical practitioner.

3.3 Maternity Leave
(a) Every female employee is entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for
the birth of the first two children. For the third child and beyond, the
entitlement is 12 weeks of paid maternity leave.

(b) A female employee who has had a miscarriage is entitled to 6 weeks of
paid leave from the date of the miscarriage.

(c) Female employees who adopt a child below 3 months of age are entitled to
12 weeks of paid adoption leave.

(d) The Company shall not terminate or alter the terms of employment of a
female employee during the period of maternity leave.

3.4 Paternity Leave
Male employees and commissioning parents are entitled to 5 working days of
paid paternity leave, to be availed within 30 days of the birth or adoption
of a child.

3.5 Crèche Facility
The Company maintains a crèche facility at all offices employing 50 or more
employees, as required under the Maternity Benefit Act. Female employees with
children below 6 years of age are entitled to 4 crèche visits per working day.
The crèche facility is also available to male employees and single parents.

CHAPTER 4 — PREVENTION OF SEXUAL HARASSMENT (POSH)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1 Zero Tolerance Policy
Zenith Corporation maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy toward sexual
harassment of any nature at the workplace. This policy applies to all
employees, vendors, clients, and visitors.

4.2 Internal Complaints Committee
In compliance with the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention,
Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013, the Company has constituted an Internal
Complaints Committee (ICC) at each of its offices. The ICC comprises:

(a) Presiding Officer: Ms. Ananya Iyer, Senior Vice President — Legal, who is
    a senior woman employee of the Company;
(b) Internal Members: Two employees committed to the cause of women — Mr.
    Rahul Mehta (People Operations) and Ms. Divya Krishnaswamy (Finance);
(c) External Member: Ms. Fatima Siddiqui, representing NGO "Naari Suraksha",
    an organisation working for the welfare of women.

4.3 Filing a Complaint
An employee who experiences sexual harassment may file a written complaint
with the Presiding Officer of the ICC within 3 months of the incident.
Complaints may be filed in person, by email, or through the confidential
POSH portal on the Company intranet.

4.4 Inquiry Procedure
Upon receipt of a complaint, the ICC shall complete its inquiry within
60 days. Both the complainant and the respondent shall be given an
opportunity to present their case. The ICC may recommend conciliation
where both parties consent.

4.5 Awareness and Training
Mandatory POSH awareness workshops are conducted every quarter for all
employees across all locations. New joiners must complete the POSH
e-learning module within 30 days of joining. The Company publishes an
Annual POSH Report to the District Officer as required by law.

4.6 Protection Against Retaliation
No employee shall be retaliated against for filing a complaint in good faith
or for participating in a POSH inquiry. Retaliation is itself a disciplinable
offence.

CHAPTER 5 — SEPARATION & INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5.1 Notice Period
Either party may terminate this employment relationship by providing 30 days
written notice, or by making payment of salary in lieu thereof. During the
notice period, the employee is expected to continue performing their duties
and support a smooth knowledge transition.

5.2 Termination for Cause
Termination for gross misconduct, fraud, or breach of fiduciary duty shall
be effected only after a fair domestic inquiry in which the employee has been
given an opportunity to be heard. The inquiry officer shall submit findings
within 30 days of initiation.

5.3 Retrenchment
Any retrenchment of employees shall be carried out in compliance with the
Industrial Relations Code 2020, including the provision of statutory notice
and retrenchment compensation as applicable.

5.4 Full and Final Settlement
All dues payable to a separated employee, including pending salary, leave
encashment, PF, gratuity, and any performance bonus accrued, shall be
settled within 30 days of the date of separation.

CHAPTER 6 — DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION POLICY

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

6.1 Commitment
Zenith Corporation is a Data Fiduciary as defined under the Digital Personal
Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"). The Company is committed to processing
personal data of all Data Principals in a lawful, fair, and transparent manner.

6.2 Consent
Personal data shall be processed only after obtaining free, specific, informed,
and unambiguous consent from the data principal in a clear and plain language.
A separate, granular consent is obtained for each distinct purpose of processing.
Employees may withdraw consent at any time by notifying the Data Protection
Officer. Withdrawal of consent shall not affect the lawfulness of processing
prior to withdrawal.

6.3 Purpose Limitation
Personal data is collected and processed only for specified, explicit, and
legitimate purposes communicated to the data principal at the time of consent.
Data shall not be used for any purpose incompatible with the original purpose
for which consent was obtained.

6.4 Data Minimisation
The Company collects only such personal data as is necessary for the specified
purpose. Employees are not required to provide data beyond what is reasonably
necessary for employment administration.

6.5 Data Principal Rights
Every employee (as a Data Principal) has the following rights under the DPDP Act:

(a) Right to Access: Employees may request a summary of their personal data
    processed by the Company and information about processing activities.

(b) Right to Correction and Erasure: Employees have the right to request
    correction of inaccurate or incomplete data, and the right to request
    erasure of personal data where such data is no longer necessary for the
    purpose for which it was collected, or where the data principal has
    withdrawn consent.

(c) Right to Grievance Redressal: Employees may raise data-related grievances
    by contacting the Data Protection Officer.

6.6 Third-Party Data Processors
Employee data may be shared with authorised data processors (payroll vendors,
insurance providers, background verification firms) strictly under Data
Processing Agreements that bind processors to confidentiality and security
obligations equivalent to those under the DPDP Act.

6.7 Data Breach Notification
In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in risk to the
rights of data principals, the Company shall notify the affected employees and
the Data Protection Board of India within 72 hours of becoming aware of the
breach. The breach notification will include the nature of the breach, data
categories affected, and remedial measures taken.

6.8 Data Retention and Deletion
Personal data shall not be retained for longer than is necessary for the
specified purpose. Upon termination of employment, personal data shall be
reviewed and securely deleted within 90 days, save for data required to be
retained under applicable law.

CHAPTER 7 — INFORMATION SECURITY

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

7.1 Reasonable Security Practices
As required under Section 43A of the Information Technology Act, 2000, the
Company implements reasonable security practices and procedures to protect
sensitive personal data and information. These include:

(a) AES-256 encryption for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit;
(b) Role-based access controls (RBAC) ensuring least-privilege access;
(c) Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all systems containing sensitive data;
(d) Annual information security audits by an independent external auditor;
(e) Incident response procedures with defined recovery time objectives.

7.2 Confidentiality Obligations
All employees must maintain strict confidentiality of Company data, client
information, and trade secrets during and after employment. Breach of
confidentiality is a serious disciplinary offence and may give rise to
civil and criminal liability under Section 72A of the IT Act, 2000.

7.3 Acceptable Use
Company IT assets, including laptops, email, and software, must be used for
legitimate business purposes only. Personal use is permitted to a reasonable
extent provided it does not compromise security or productivity.

CHAPTER 9 — GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

9.1 General Grievances
Employees may raise grievances related to employment conditions through
the HR helpdesk. All grievances will be acknowledged within 3 working days
and resolved within 15 working days.

9.2 Data Protection Officer
The Company has appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO) to oversee
compliance with the DPDP Act. Employees may contact the DPO directly for
data-related concerns: dpo@zenithcorp.in

9.3 Escalation
Unresolved grievances may be escalated to the Chief People Officer and
thereafter to the Audit Committee of the Board.
