1. INTRODUCTION

TechNova Pvt. Ltd. is a technology startup based in Bangalore with approximately 50
employees. This handbook sets out the basic terms and conditions of employment. All
employees are expected to read and comply with these policies.

2. LEAVE POLICY

2.1 Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to leave as determined by the management from time to time.
Leave allocation may change based on company performance and business needs.
Leave must be approved by the reporting manager in advance.

2.2 Sick Leave
Sick leave of up to 7 days per year may be granted at management discretion.
Additional sick leave beyond 7 days will be treated as leave without pay (LWP).

2.3 Maternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to maternity leave of 12 weeks (84 days).
Maternity leave is available for female employees who have completed 6 months
of continuous service. No paternity leave is provided by the company.

2.4 Leave Without Pay
Any leave beyond sanctioned limits will be treated as leave without pay. The
management reserves the right to reject any leave application if business
requirements demand so.

3. WORKING HOURS & ATTENDANCE

3.1 Standard Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Saturday.
Flexible working hours may be arranged with manager approval.

3.2 Attendance
Employees are expected to maintain regular attendance. Three or more unexcused
absences in a month may result in disciplinary action.

3.3 Overtime
Overtime work may be required as per project needs. Compensation for overtime
will be decided by management on a case-by-case basis.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4. COMPENSATION & BENEFITS

4.1 Salary
Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month. The company reserves
the right to revise salaries based on performance appraisals.

4.2 Provident Fund
PF deductions will be made as per statutory requirements. The company's
contribution will be as per government mandates.

4.3 Insurance
Group health insurance is provided to all full-time employees.

5. DATA HANDLING & EMPLOYEE INFORMATION

5.1 Collection of Employee Data
The company collects employee personal information including name, address,
bank details, PAN, Aadhaar, and contact information for employment and
administrative purposes.

5.2 Use and Sharing of Data
Employee data may be shared with third-party vendors and service providers
for payroll processing, insurance administration, background verification,
and other operational requirements. Employees are deemed to consent to such
sharing upon joining the company.

5.3 Data Retention
Employee records will be maintained as per company requirements. The company
will retain employee data for as long as deemed necessary for business operations.

5.4 Data Security
Data is stored on company servers. Standard practices are followed to maintain
data security. Employees must not share login credentials with others.

6. CODE OF CONDUCT

6.1 Professional Behaviour
All employees are expected to maintain professional conduct in the workplace.
TechNova promotes a respectful and collaborative work environment.

6.2 Anti-Discrimination
The company does not discriminate on the basis of gender, religion, caste,
or disability. All employees must treat colleagues with respect.

6.3 Confidentiality
Employees must maintain confidentiality of all company information, client
data, and business plans during and after their employment.

7. SEPARATION & TERMINATION

7.1 Resignation
Employees wishing to resign must inform HR in writing. The management will
determine the handover period based on project commitments.

7.2 Termination
The company may terminate the employment of any employee at any time for
cause or otherwise, as deemed appropriate by the management. The company
will endeavour to provide reasonable notice where possible.

7.3 Exit Process
Upon separation, employees must return all company property, complete
knowledge transfer, and settle any outstanding dues.

8. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL

8.1 Complaints
Employees may raise concerns informally with their reporting manager or
directly with HR. The company will review complaints at its discretion.
