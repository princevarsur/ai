PART A — EMPLOYMENT POLICY

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY

1.1 This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, and contractual employees
of Innovate Solutions India Pvt. Ltd. (hereinafter "the Company").

1.2 The Company is committed to compliance with applicable Indian labour and
employment laws, including but not limited to the Labour Codes of 2020.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2. LEAVE ENTITLEMENTS

2.1 Annual & Casual Leave
Employees are entitled to 24 days of paid leave per calendar year,
comprising 12 days of earned leave, 8 days of casual leave, and
4 days of optional holidays.

2.2 Sick Leave
All employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
Sick leave in excess of 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.

2.3 Maternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to maternity leave as per applicable law.
Maternity leave applications must be submitted to HR at least 4 weeks
in advance. The leave covers the pre-natal and post-natal period as
specified under the Maternity Benefit Act.

2.4 Paternity Leave
Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave to be
availed within 30 days of the birth of a child.

2.5 Leave Encashment
Unused earned leave up to a maximum of 30 days may be encashed
at the time of separation.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3. COMPENSATION & STATUTORY BENEFITS

3.1 Salary Payment
Wages are paid on the 7th of each subsequent month via direct bank transfer
to the employee's registered bank account.

3.2 Provident Fund
The Company deducts Provident Fund (PF) contributions at 12% of basic wages
from employee salaries and contributes an equal amount as employer
contribution. PF is deposited with the EPFO on a monthly basis.

3.3 Gratuity
Employees who have completed 5 years of continuous service are eligible
for gratuity as per the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.

3.4 Performance Bonuses
Performance bonuses are declared annually based on company results and
individual appraisal ratings.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4. WORKING CONDITIONS

4.1 Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday,
with a 1-hour lunch break. Saturday attendance may be required based
on project deadlines.

4.2 Remote Work
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with prior approval
from their reporting manager.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5. SEPARATION

5.1 Resignation
An employee wishing to resign must give 30 days written notice to the Company
or pay salary in lieu of notice. The Company shall also provide 30 days notice
or payment in lieu thereof in the event of termination without cause.

5.2 Termination for Cause
In cases of gross misconduct, the Company may terminate employment without
notice following a fair inquiry procedure.

5.3 Exit Settlement
All dues, including pending salary, leave encashment, and gratuity (where
applicable), shall be settled within 30 days of separation.

6. ANTI-HARASSMENT POLICY

6.1 Commitment
The Company is committed to providing a safe, dignified, and harassment-free
workplace for all employees, regardless of gender, caste, religion, or
disability.

6.2 Prohibited Conduct
Sexual harassment, including unwelcome physical contact, sexually coloured
remarks, requests for sexual favours, and display of pornographic material,
is strictly prohibited.

6.3 Complaint Mechanism
Employees who experience harassment should report it to the HR department.
The HR department will investigate all complaints in a fair and confidential
manner and take appropriate action.

6.4 Annual Awareness
The Company conducts annual awareness sessions on prevention of workplace
harassment for all employees.

PART C — DATA GOVERNANCE POLICY

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

7. DATA COLLECTION & CONSENT

7.1 Collection
The Company collects personal data from employees including name, contact
details, identification documents, and financial information for employment
administration purposes.

7.2 Consent
The Company will obtain consent from employees before processing their
personal data for purposes beyond those directly related to employment.
Employees are informed about data collection at the time of onboarding.

7.3 Third-Party Sharing
Employee data may be shared with payroll vendors, insurance providers,
and regulatory bodies. Such sharing is limited to what is necessary for
the specific purpose.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

8. DATA SECURITY

8.1 Security Measures
The Company implements appropriate technical and organisational measures
to protect employee data from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.
Access to employee data is restricted to authorised personnel only.

8.2 Breach Response
In the event of a data security incident, the Company will take immediate
steps to contain and investigate the breach.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

9. EMPLOYEE DATA RIGHTS

9.1 Access and Correction
Employees may request access to their personal data held by the Company
and request correction of any inaccuracies by contacting HR.

9.2 Grievance Redressal
Employees with data-related concerns may raise them with the HR department,
which will respond within 15 working days.
